## 📌 Featured Projects

### [Medical AI Failure Analysis | Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct](https://github.com/Sheneal251/-Medical-AI-Failure-Analysis)

Evaluated an open-source language model using 30 medical questions from the MedQuAD dataset. Compared generated responses with reference answers and identified errors involving genetic inheritance and medical procedure recommendations. Tested different question phrasings to examine how response accuracy and consistency changed.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Hugging Face Transformers, Qwen2.5, Google Colab

### [Wealth Management Client Portfolio & Retention Dashboard](https://github.com/Sheneal251/wealth-management-client-retention-powerbi)

Created a two-page Power BI dashboard using 10,000 public customer records. Cleaned and validated the data in Power Query, developed customer segments, and used DAX measures to track net assets, portfolio returns, product adoption, retention, and churn.

**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel

### [Urban Air Quality & Climate Analysis](https://github.com/Sheneal251/Urban-Air-Quality)

Examined environmental and weather data across 10 U.S. cities using Python, SQL, Excel, and Tableau. Identified heat index as the strongest predictor associated with changes in Heat Risk Score and presented geographic and weather-related patterns through visualizations.

**Tools:** Python, SQL, Excel, Tableau

### [NYCHA Utility Consumption & Energy Analysis](https://github.com/Sheneal251/NYCHA-Utility-Consumption-Energy-Analysis-)

Designed an interactive Tableau dashboard to explore steam, water, electricity, and heating gas consumption. Added utility-selection parameters, KPI summaries, conditional tables, and borough-level comparisons to make consumption patterns easier to understand.

**Tools:** Tableau, Excel, Data Cleaning, KPI Analysis
